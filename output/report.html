Methodology & assumptions

Every figure comes from your two CSV files. The rules below are judgement calls that can be changed in analyzer/config.py.

  • Target ROAS 3.0×; a campaign is "weak" if it is below target, or well below the non-brand average with a large budget share (≥ 10%).
  • Organic CTR is compared with a benchmark CTR for each ranking position (an assumption, not your own data).
  • "Page 2" pages are those at positions 8–20 with at least 5,000 impressions.
  • An organic click is valued at 50% of an average paid click ($1), and only half of any modelled gain is counted.
  • Trends compare this run with the previous run on different data; the CSVs themselves contain no dates.
